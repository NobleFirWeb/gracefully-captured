Meet your Photographer

Hello,
I'm Grace

I have always had a love for photography. I believe photos are a way we can look back on our lives as if we are still in that moment.

I focus on making a fun and safe space for my clients to be themselves — to capture their perfect moment. I've been a professional photographer since January 2021, and my work ranges from senior portraits to something you saw on Pinterest.

Show me your vision and I'll make it happen. I can't wait to capture your special moment in life.
